What architectural film does

Heat & glare rejection

Spectrally-selective film turns down the solar heat on west- and south-facing glass while staying nearly clear, so a room stops overheating in the afternoon without losing its daylight. Less heat gain means a lower cooling load all summer.

UV protection

Quality film blocks up to 99% of ultraviolet light, the main cause of fading in wood floors, upholstery, artwork, and retail merchandise near the glass.

Privacy & decorative film

Frosted, etched-glass, gradient, and patterned films add privacy to conference rooms, bathrooms, and street-facing glass, or brand a lobby, without bricking up the window.

Security & safety film

Thick, tear-resistant film holds broken glass in the frame after an impact — slowing forced entry, containing storm and accident damage, and helping meet safety-glazing goals on older glass.

Anti-graffiti film

A clear sacrificial layer on storefront and ground-floor glass: if it gets etched or tagged, the film is peeled and replaced for a fraction of the cost of the glass.

We check your glass first

Dual-pane, low-e, tempered, laminated, and single-pane annealed glass each handle absorbed heat differently. The wrong film on the wrong glass is the most common cause of thermal-stress cracks and voided window warranties, so every quote starts with us identifying the glass on-site and matching it to the film manufacturer's compatibility chart. If a window isn't a safe candidate, we tell you before you commit.

Window Film FAQ

What is architectural window film?

A thin, optically clear film applied to existing flat glass that can reject heat, cut glare, block UV, add privacy, hold broken glass together, or create a decorative look — without replacing the window.

Will it make my rooms dark?

Not with a spectrally-selective heat film, which stays nearly clear. Darker and reflective films are options when privacy or a specific look is the point.

Does film help with energy bills?

Yes — it lowers the cooling load on the glass that gains the most summer heat, and evens out hot and cold spots so a space is comfortable at the same thermostat setting.

Why do you check the glass before quoting?

Because the wrong film on the wrong glass can cause thermal-stress cracks or void the window's seal warranty. We match your specific glass to the film manufacturer's chart first.
